## 1. What Tabi is — and isn't

Tabi is a medication reminder and tracking app. It helps you keep a schedule,
log doses, and optionally share your progress with people you choose.

**Tabi is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice.** Nothing
in the app — including information extracted from a scanned label, dosage
suggestions, or drug-interaction information — is a substitute for the advice
of a licensed physician, pharmacist, or other qualified healthcare provider.
Always follow your prescriber's and pharmacist's instructions over anything
shown in the app. If you believe you're experiencing a medical emergency, call
911 (or your local emergency number) immediately — do not rely on Tabi.

**Label-scanning accuracy.** Tabi uses on-device text recognition and an AI
service (Google Gemini) to read medication names, dosage, and schedule from a
photo of a prescription label. This process can make mistakes — misread text,
wrong dosage, or an incorrect schedule. **You are responsible for reviewing
and confirming every detail Tabi extracts before saving a medication**, and
for verifying it against your actual prescription and pharmacy label.

## 4. Sharing your information with others

Tabi lets you invite other people ("**caretakers**") to see your medication
schedule and dose status, and be notified if you miss a dose. By adding a
caretaker, you're authorizing Tabi to share that information with them once
they confirm the connection. You can remove a caretaker's access at any time,
which stops future sharing but does not retroactively un-share information
they already received.

If you invite a caretaker or request access to someone else's medication list,
you confirm you have the right to share the contact information (phone number
or email) you provide for that purpose.

## 5. SMS messages

Tabi sends text messages for connection confirmations and missed-dose alerts.
Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any Tabi text to stop
receiving messages, or HELP for help. Opting out of SMS as a caretaker means
you will no longer receive missed-dose alerts for that connection.
