# Tabi Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** [DATE — not yet published]
**Last updated:** [DATE]

Tabi ("**Tabi**," "**we**," "**us**") provides a mobile app that helps you track
medications, get dose reminders, and optionally share your progress with people
you choose. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we
use it, who we share it with, and the choices and rights you have.

This policy covers the Tabi iOS app. It does not cover third-party services we
link to or integrate with, which have their own privacy policies (see
"Third-party services" below).

## 1. Information we collect

**Account information.** When you create a Tabi account, we collect your name
and, depending on how you sign in, an email address (via Sign in with Apple or
Google). We use Firebase Authentication to manage sign-in; we don't see or
store your password.

**Medication and health information.** The core purpose of Tabi is to store:
- Medication names, dosage, and schedule
- Dose history (when you marked a dose taken, skipped, or missed)
- Allergies you choose to record, including severity and symptoms
- Pharmacy information you choose to add (name, address, pharmacist, phone)

**Optional profile information.** You may optionally provide your age, gender,
height, weight, and a profile photo. None of these are required to use Tabi,
and leaving them blank does not limit any core feature.

**Photos.** When you scan a prescription label to add a medication, or take a
photo to log a dose, that photo is processed to read the label or verify the
pill. The photo itself is processed on your device; only the text extracted
from a prescription label is sent to our AI provider to structure it into a
medication name, dosage, and schedule (see "Third-party services" below).

**Caretaker/sharing information.** If you invite someone as a caretaker, we
collect the phone number or email you provide for them so we can send a
confirmation request. If you ask someone else to share their medication list
with you, we collect the contact information you enter for that request.

**Location.** If you search for a pharmacy by name or address, we use your
approximate location (with your permission) to show relevant results. We do
not track or store your location history.

**Technical information.** Standard technical data needed to operate the app
and diagnose problems — device type, OS version, and crash/error logs.

**We do not collect:** precise ongoing location tracking, browsing history
outside the app, or data from your contacts beyond the specific contact you
choose to enter when inviting a caretaker or requesting shared access.

## 2. How we use your information

We use your information to:
- Run your medication reminders and dose tracking
- Show your adherence progress and history
- Let you optionally connect with caretakers or people you've chosen to share
  with — and let them see your medication schedule and dose status once *they*
  confirm the connection
- Extract structured medication details from a scanned prescription label
- Operate, secure, and improve the app (bug fixes, performance)
- Communicate with you about your account, including SMS alerts you or a
  caretaker you've added have opted into

**We do not use your health data for advertising, and we do not sell it.**
We do not use ad networks or third-party analytics/advertising SDKs in Tabi.

## 3. How we share your information

We share information only in these circumstances:

**With people you invite.** If you add a caretaker, they can see your
medication names, schedule, and whether you've taken a dose, and they'll be
notified if you miss one — but only after they've independently confirmed the
connection via a text message we send them. You can remove a caretaker's
access at any time in the Sharing tab.

**With service providers who help us run Tabi**, under contracts that limit
their use of your data to providing the service to us:
- **Our cloud hosting provider** — hosts your account and app data
- **An AI service provider** — receives the *text* extracted from a scanned
  prescription label (not the photo) to identify the medication name, dosage,
  and schedule
- **Our SMS delivery provider** — delivers the text messages used for
  caretaker invitations and missed-dose alerts

The specific companies we currently use for each of these are listed in
"Third-party services" below — kept in one place so it stays accurate if we
ever change providers.

**For legal reasons**, if required to comply with a valid legal process, or to
protect the rights, safety, or property of Tabi, our users, or the public.

**In a business transfer**, if Tabi is involved in a merger, acquisition, or
sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that deal,
subject to this policy or a policy at least as protective.

**We do not sell your personal information**, and we do not share it with
advertisers or data brokers.

**Future research or analytics use.** We do not currently share your data,
even in de-identified or aggregate form, with pharmaceutical, research, or
other commercial partners. If that ever changes, we will update this policy,
notify you directly, and ask for your affirmative opt-in before any of your
data is used that way — participation will never be required to use Tabi's
core features.

## 4. Your choices and rights

**Delete your account and data.** You can permanently delete your account and
all associated data at any time in Settings → Privacy → Delete My Account &
Data. This removes your medications, dose history, and shared connections. It
cannot be undone.

**Access and correction.** You can view and edit most of your information
directly in the app (Profile → Edit Profile). To request a copy of your data
or ask us to correct something you can't edit yourself, contact us at
[CONTACT EMAIL].

**Caretaker opt-out.** Anyone you invite as a caretaker can decline the
confirmation request, or reply STOP to any Tabi text message to stop receiving
SMS from us.

**California residents** have rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right
to know what personal information we collect, request deletion, correct
inaccurate information, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal
information. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are
defined by California law. To exercise these rights, contact us at
[CONTACT EMAIL].

**Washington, Nevada, and Connecticut residents** have rights under consumer
health data laws (e.g., Washington's My Health My Data Act), including the
right to access, delete, and withdraw consent for the collection or sharing of
consumer health data, and the right to a list of third parties your data has
been shared with upon request. We do not sell consumer health data.

**California medical information (CMIA).** As a California resident, you have
rights under the Confidentiality of Medical Information Act, including the
right to control disclosure of your medical information to third parties.

## 6. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including
encryption in transit and at rest (via Firebase's infrastructure) and access
controls that restrict your data to your own account. No method of storage or
transmission is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Children's privacy

and we do not knowingly collect information from children under that age. If
you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at
